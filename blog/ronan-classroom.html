A signed letter and a box of T-shirts for a classroom in Ronan

A class far from home loved my book, so I sent them something back.

By Jessica Sellars

Some of my favorite readers live a long way from home. My sister-in-law teaches a class in Ronan, and when she shared my book with her students, they loved it so much that it stayed with me for days.

So I sent the whole class a signed letter and a box of Echo T-shirts.

Why this class

Ronan is far from Oregon, but I have family there, and that makes it feel a little closer. I’ve watched my sister-in-law with her students, and I see how much love and support she gives her classroom. Those kids are lucky to have her.

Hearing that they loved my book warmed my heart. I write these stories hoping they’ll find the kids who need them, and it means the world when they do.

Giving back

I wanted to give something back to that community. A signed letter felt personal, a thank-you straight from the author. The T-shirts are something they can wear and keep, a little piece of the story to take home.

To the class in Ronan: thank you for loving my book so much. Keep reading!
