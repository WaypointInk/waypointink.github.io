The fiction of J.R. Deem

Somber, strange, and quietly certain something is real.

J.R. Deem writes slipstream fiction and YA/NA fantasy about people tethered to each other in ways that outlast memory, geography, and sometimes death. Currently in progress: three long-form works, side by side.

First draft complete

Static

After the crash that should have killed them both, Eli wakes up to rebuild a life that no longer fits — a fiancée who's already gone, a memory that won't hold still, and a feeling he can't shake that some part of that night never actually ended. Twenty years, two cats named Static and Void, and a quiet certainty that something didn't add up.

Slipstream novella
In progress

Where the Ley Runs Dry

Sorrel, Roan, and Fen cross a post-apocalyptic fantasy landscape where the magic itself is running out. A slow-burn romance threads through a 27-chapter adventure toward an ~87,000-word finish.

YA / NA fantasy
Coming late 2029

Where the Ash Blooms

Two hundred years after the mountain buried the world in ash, an underground forager and a surface scout are drawn together by dreams neither of them asked for — and a dying man's notebook that says the mountain isn't finished yet.

Post-apocalyptic steampunk romantasy · Book One of The Emberhill Chronicles
