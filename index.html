The fiction of J.R. Deem

Somber, strange, and quietly certain something is real.

J.R. Deem writes slipstream fiction and YA/NA fantasy about people tethered to each other in ways that outlast memory, geography, and sometimes death. Currently in progress: three long-form works, side by side.

Static

After the crash that should have killed them both, Eli wakes up to rebuild a life that no longer fits — a fiancée who's already gone, a memory that won't hold still, and a feeling he can't shake that some part of that night never actually ended. Twenty years, two cats named Static and Void, and a quiet certainty that something didn't add up.
