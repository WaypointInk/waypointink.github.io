The fiction of J.R. Deem

Somber, strange, and quietly certain something is real.

J.R. Deem writes slipstream fiction and YA/NA fantasy about people tethered to each other in ways that outlast memory, geography, and sometimes death. Currently in progress: three long-form works, side by side.
