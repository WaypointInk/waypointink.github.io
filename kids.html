Meet the real dogs

Breed facts for curious kids

Echo and Helix are real dogs — here's a little bit about the breeds behind the books.

J

Korean Jindo

Echo's breed

Where they're from: Jindos are named for Jindo Island, off the coast of South Korea, where the breed has lived for centuries.

Size: Jindos are medium-sized dogs — usually 18 to 22 inches tall and 35 to 60 pounds full grown.

Super smart: Jindos are quick learners and can pick up even tricky tricks and agility courses with ease.

Extremely loyal: Jindos bond closely with their families and are known as some of the most devoted dogs of any breed.

Clean like a cat: Jindos groom themselves and keep their own coats tidy, so they need very little bathing.

Long life: A healthy Jindo can live 12 to 15 years.

Echo is a real Jindo, rescued from Korea — the same journey that inspired her stories.

H

Chihuahua

Helix's breed

Ancient breed: Chihuahuas are one of the oldest breeds in the Americas, with roots going back to ancient Mexico.

National symbol: The Chihuahua is considered a national symbol of Mexico, named after the Mexican state of Chihuahua.

Tiny size: Chihuahuas are one of the smallest dog breeds in the world, usually under 6 pounds full grown.

Big personality: Despite their small size, Chihuahuas are bold, alert, and full of confidence — a huge personality in a tiny body.

Great watchdogs: Chihuahuas are very vocal and will let you know right away when someone's at the door.

Long life: Chihuahuas often live 14 to 16 years — one of the longest lifespans of any dog breed.

Helix is a real five-pound chihuahua and Echo's real-life best friend.
