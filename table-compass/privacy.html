Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

The short version: Table Compass doesn't collect, sell, or share your information. Everything you put in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your recipes, recipe photos, meal plans, shopping lists, prices, receipt details, budget, and settings are saved only on the device where you use Table Compass. We (Waypoint Ink) never receive a copy.

Receipts and photos

When you scan a receipt or a recipe card, the photo is read on your device. The text and prices it finds are saved in the app, and the photo is not sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking tools
  • No analytics about how you use the app
  • No selling or sharing of your information

Reminders

If you turn on "start cooking" reminders, the app schedules them on your own device. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Purchases

Table Compass is sold through app stores such as the Microsoft Store. The store handles your payment under its own privacy policy, and we don't receive your payment details.

Deleting your information

Because your information lives only on your device, you're in control. Delete individual items in the app, or uninstall Table Compass to remove everything.

Children

Table Compass is a household planning tool meant for adults. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date above.
