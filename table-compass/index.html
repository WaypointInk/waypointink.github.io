What it does

Plans your week

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner for every day. Mark eat-out nights and leftovers, and swap any meal in a tap.

Your own recipes

Snap a photo of a recipe card or type it in. Allergy settings keep the wrong foods out of your plan.

A smarter shopping list

It builds itself from the week's meals and shows what you paid last time and where it was cheaper.

A weekly budget

Scan your receipts and the list estimates this week's trip, so you know before you go if you're on budget.

"Start cooking" reminders

Tell it when you want to eat. It counts back from each recipe's cook time and can nudge you when it's time to start.

Menus worth printing

Clean weekly menus with ingredient lists, ready for the fridge door.

Cook mode

Big, clear steps that keep your screen awake while your hands are busy.

Make it yours

Light or dark mode, and every feature can be turned off. Keep only what helps you.

Made for busy households

Table Compass was built by a working parent who was tired of the 5 p.m. "what's for dinner?" scramble. It's friendly for beginner cooks and for brains that like things written down.
