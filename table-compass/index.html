Who it's for

Busy parents

ADHD and busy brains

Meal planning is dozens of small decisions and things to remember, and they all land at once. Turn off any feature and keep it simple.

Allergies and special diets

Set it once and it never plans a meal that doesn't fit. Works with food allergies, vegetarian and vegan, keto, low sodium, low sugar, paleo, Whole30, low fat, halal, and kosher-style.

Home gardeners

Grow zucchini, tomatoes, or a hydroponic herb garden? Log what you pick and it plans meals around your harvest, warns you before it goes bad, and shows you how to freeze, can, or pickle the rest.

New cooks

Clear recipes, simple steps, timers, and even kid-sized kitchen jobs so the little ones can help.

The little things it remembers for you

  • "Pull the chicken from the freezer today." The day before a meal that uses frozen meat, it reminds you to move it to the fridge, and the reminder prints on the fridge menu too.
  • "Start cooking by 5:10." Tell it when you want to eat. It counts back from each recipe's cook time and can nudge you when it's time to start.
  • Eating out tonight? Push the rest of the week back a night so the groceries you bought still get used. Nothing goes to waste.
  • Milk every week. Scan your receipts and it learns what you buy most weeks and adds it to every list for you.
  • Groceries you just bought get used first. It plans the fish and fresh chicken early in the week, before they spoil.
  • What you paid last time. Your list shows past prices, where it was cheaper, and whether this week's trip fits your budget.

Everything it does

Your own recipes

A shopping list that writes itself
