Common questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Table Compass is $9.99 one time, with every feature and free updates.

How do I add my own recipe?

Go to Recipes → Add. Take a photo of a recipe card or page, or type it in. Check the ingredients it found, then save.

How do the prices on my shopping list work?

Scan a grocery receipt (Shopping list → Scan receipt). Table Compass remembers each price, store, and date. Next time that item is on your list, it shows what you paid and where it was cheaper. You can also tap + $ on any item to type a price.

How do I set a weekly budget?

At the top of the shopping list, tap Set a weekly budget. The list then shows the estimated total and how much you have left.

How do "start cooking" reminders work?

Set your usual dinner time in Settings → Dinner time. Each dinner shows when to start, based on its cook time. Turn on the alert there to get a reminder on time or 10, 20, or 30 minutes early. To eat later just one night, use that day's ⋯ menu.

Can I print my menu?

Yes. Open the week and choose Print. You'll get the menu and a shopping list with ingredients and estimated prices.

How do I switch to dark mode or hide features I don't use?

Settings → Household → Appearance for light or dark. Settings → Show or hide features to turn any feature off.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your device. See our privacy policy.
