Echo's ABC

by Jessica Sellars

From A for Adoption to Z for Zoomies, Echo — a real Jindo rescued from Korea — walks little readers through the alphabet one very dog-shaped day at a time.

A warm, funny first-words book about a new home, a best friend, and all the small joys (and a few indignities) of being a family dog.
